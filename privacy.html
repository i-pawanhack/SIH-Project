PRIVACY POLICY

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when using the AI-assisted retinal screening platform.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on deployment configuration, the system may process:

  • Fundus/retinal images.
  • Patient or case identifiers.
  • Screening metadata.
  • Image-quality results.
  • DR prediction.
  • Lesion findings.
  • Retinal structure information.
  • Confidence scores.
  • Uncertainty indicators.
  • Explainability information.
  • Triage/referral status.
  • Generated screening reports.

3. Retinal Images Are Sensitive Information

Retinal images can contain sensitive healthcare information and should be handled accordingly.

Users and deployment administrators should ensure that images are collected, transmitted, stored, and accessed according to applicable institutional policies and laws.

4. Data Minimization

Only information necessary for the intended screening workflow should be collected.

Avoid unnecessarily collecting:

  • Personal identifiers.
  • Contact information.
  • Government identification numbers.
  • Unrelated medical information.

5. Use of Data

Information processed by the platform may be used for:

  • Retinal image analysis.
  • DR screening.
  • Lesion analysis.
  • Explainable prediction.
  • Uncertainty assessment.
  • Screening report generation.
  • Human-review workflows.
  • System testing and research, where appropriately authorized.

6. Data Storage

Storage depends on the specific deployment configuration.

The platform documentation should not be interpreted as guaranteeing permanent storage, automatic deletion, or zero-storage processing unless that behaviour is actually implemented in the deployed system.

7. Data Security

Deployment administrators should implement appropriate safeguards, including:

  • Authentication.
  • Role-based access.
  • Secure communication.
  • Controlled database access.
  • Secure image storage.
  • Access logging where applicable.
  • Protection against unauthorized downloads.

8. Patient Identification

Patient-identifiable information should not be unnecessarily displayed in:

  • Public URLs.
  • Screenshots.
  • Logs.
  • Demonstration videos.
  • Public repositories.
  • Publicly shared reports.

9. Third-Party Services

If the deployed version uses external cloud services, APIs, storage providers, or healthcare systems, their respective privacy policies and data-processing terms may also apply.

10. Research & Dataset Data

Public research datasets used during development may have their own licenses, terms of use, and restrictions. Dataset-specific requirements must be followed separately.

11. User Responsibility

Organizations deploying the platform are responsible for determining:

  • What patient information is collected.
  • Where it is stored.
  • Who can access it.
  • How long it is retained.
  • When it is deleted.
  • Which healthcare/privacy regulations apply to their deployment.

12. Privacy by Design

The platform is intended to support a workflow where sensitive patient information is minimized and access is restricted to authorized users.
