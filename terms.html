1. About the Platform

This platform is an AI-assisted Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) screening and triage research prototype developed under SIH26038 – Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India.

The platform uses retinal image processing, computer vision, machine learning, explainable AI, uncertainty estimation, and automated reporting to assist healthcare screening workflows.

2. Intended Purpose

The platform is intended to:

  • Assess retinal/fundus image quality.
  • Enhance retinal images for computational analysis.
  • Analyse retinal structures.
  • Detect potential retinal lesions.
  • Estimate DR severity from Grade 0 to Grade 4.
  • Provide explainable evidence associated with an AI prediction.
  • Provide confidence and uncertainty information.
  • Generate a structured screening report.
  • Support screening triage and referral workflows.

3. Medical Disclaimer

The platform does not provide a final medical diagnosis.

AI-generated results are intended only as decision-support information and must not replace examination, diagnosis, or treatment decisions made by a qualified ophthalmologist or other appropriate healthcare professional.

4. Image Quality Assessment

Every submitted image may undergo an image-quality assessment before DR analysis.

If an image is considered insufficient or ungradable, the platform may return:

RECAPTURE_REQUIRED

In such cases, the system should not be interpreted as having diagnosed the patient.

5. AI Screening & Triage

The platform may assign a workflow status such as:

  • AUTO_SCREEN — suitable for automated screening workflow under the configured system criteria.
  • HUMAN_REVIEW — requires review by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
  • RECAPTURE_REQUIRED — image quality is insufficient for reliable analysis.

These statuses represent system workflow decisions, not independent medical diagnoses.

6. DR Severity

The system is designed to work with five DR severity categories:

  • Grade 0 — No DR
  • Grade 1 — Mild DR
  • Grade 2 — Moderate DR
  • Grade 3 — Severe DR
  • Grade 4 — Proliferative DR

The predicted grade should always be interpreted together with the system's confidence, uncertainty, image quality, and explainability information.

7. Explainability

The platform may provide:

  • Feature contribution information.
  • Lesion-grounded evidence.
  • Retinal structure information.
  • Optic-disc/fovea annotations.
  • Evidence visualizations.

These visualizations explain aspects of the model's computational reasoning; they do not constitute clinical proof of disease.

8. Uncertainty & Human Review

The platform incorporates confidence, prediction margin, novelty/uncertainty indicators, and image-quality information.

Cases exceeding configured review criteria may be routed to:

HUMAN_REVIEW

A human reviewer should make the final clinical assessment.

9. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Providing appropriate retinal images.
  • Using compatible imaging equipment.
  • Ensuring images correspond to the correct screening case.
  • Following image recapture instructions.
  • Protecting patient information.
  • Referring appropriate cases to qualified healthcare professionals.

10. No Guarantee of Clinical Accuracy

System performance can vary depending on:

  • Image quality.
  • Camera/device characteristics.
  • Patient population.
  • Disease distribution.
  • Imaging conditions.
  • Dataset/domain differences.

The current prototype has not undergone external clinical validation and should not be treated as clinically validated software.
